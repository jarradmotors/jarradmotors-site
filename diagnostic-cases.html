Real Jobs We’ve Fixed

These examples show the type of diagnostic work we carry out for local drivers, work vehicles and breakdown follow-up repairs. Fault codes and symptoms are only the starting point — the important part is proving the cause before replacing parts.

Intermittent No-Start – Ford Ranger

Problem: The vehicle would randomly not start, and previous attempts to fix it had not solved the issue.

Diagnosis: Electrical testing traced the fault to the starter control circuit rather than simply guessing at a battery or starter motor.

Fix: The wiring fault was repaired and the starting system was retested to confirm reliable operation.

Engine Light & Limp Mode – Holden

Problem: The engine light was on and the vehicle had reduced power.

Diagnosis: Scan tool fault codes were used as a guide, then live data testing confirmed the issue before parts were replaced.

Fix: The faulty component was replaced and the system was checked again to make sure the fault was resolved.

Battery Going Flat Overnight

Problem: The battery kept going flat even after charging.

Diagnosis: A parasitic draw test found a module staying active when the vehicle should have been asleep.

Fix: The draw was identified and resolved, preventing repeat flat battery failures.

Airbag Warning & No-Start Fault

Problem: The vehicle had multiple warning codes and would not start correctly.

Diagnosis: Wiring diagrams, fuse checks and scan data were used to narrow the issue down instead of clearing codes and hoping.

Fix: The circuit fault was corrected, then the remaining airbag connection fault was repaired and verified.
